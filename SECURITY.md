# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

| Version | Supported |
|---------|-----------|
| Latest release (main branch) | Yes |
| Older releases | No |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Contact**: security@mereka.io

**Do NOT** open a public GitHub issue for security vulnerabilities.

### Response Timeline

| Severity | Acknowledgement | Triage | Fix Target |
|----------|----------------|--------|------------|
| Critical | 24 hours | 48 hours | 7 days |
| High | 48 hours | 5 business days | 30 days |
| Medium | 48 hours | 10 business days | 90 days |
| Low | 5 business days | 30 business days | Best effort |

### Scope

The following repositories are covered by this policy:

- [mereka-lms](https://github.com/Biji-Biji-Initiative/mereka-lms)
- [bbi-infrastructure](https://github.com/Biji-Biji-Initiative/bbi-infrastructure)
- [platform-control-plane](https://github.com/Biji-Biji-Initiative/platform-control-plane)

### Out of Scope

- Upstream Open edX platform vulnerabilities — report to [openedx.org](https://openedx.org/security/)
- Third-party dependencies — report to the respective maintainers
- Social engineering attacks
- Denial of service attacks

### What to Include

- Description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce
- Potential impact
- Suggested fix (if any)

### Safe Harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who follow this policy and report vulnerabilities responsibly.
