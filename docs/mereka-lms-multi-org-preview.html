  • Understand the principles of circular economy and how it differs from linear models

Sustainability Leadership

A joint program by Biji-Biji Initiative and IKEA. Build the knowledge and skills to lead sustainability initiatives from community to global scale.

signal_cellular_alt Beginner schedule 8-12 weeks school 4 courses
Co-created by BB Biji-Biji Initiative × IK IKEA

About this pathway

This program is jointly created by Biji-Biji Initiative and IKEA — combining Biji-Biji's grassroots circular economy expertise with IKEA's global sustainable supply chain practices. All four courses are co-authored by both organizations.

Whether you are a professional looking to integrate sustainability into your work, or a community leader seeking actionable frameworks, this pathway provides the tools you need — from local community projects to global retail sustainability.

What you'll achieve

  • check_circle Understand the science and urgency of climate change
  • check_circle Identify green career opportunities across industries
  • check_circle Apply circular economy principles to real products and services
  • check_circle Design and lead a community sustainability project
  • check_circle Earn a verified Sustainability Leadership credential
